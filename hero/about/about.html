About Ingenio

Ingenio is an all-in-one A-level and GCSE Maths revision website, made by students, for students.

Why we made it

As Year 10 students revising for our GCSEs, we hated the "free but not free" revision sites everywhere, that marketed themselves as free, when in reality every feature was locked behind a paywall. Things like "three free questions per day" plagued so many maths sites. So we built the thing we wanted: Ingenio.

What you get

  • PowerPoint Lessons for AQA, Edexcel and OCR GCSE and A-level Maths
  • Unlimited practice questions
  • Unlimited practice papers
  • Grade tracking
  • Instant marking
  • AI tutor*

Free vs Premium

Ingenio is free to start. All revision content, such as lessons, practice questions + papers, instant marking and grade tracking is free for good. However, we use ads to keep it that way. Premium removes the daily 20-prompt limit from the AI tutor, and gets rid of ads. It's also a wonderful way to support us!

* Limits apply. On free, you get 20 AI tutor prompts per day. On premium, you get unlimited AI prompts.
